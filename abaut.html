Choosing furniture

What to look for.

A few useful things to consider before choosing a piece.

01

Comfort

Think about how you will use the piece. A dining chair, a desk chair, and a sofa each need a different balance of support and softness.

02

Size and layout

Measure your room before ordering. Leave space for doors to open and chairs to pull out, and check the route from your front door.

03

Materials

Look at the finish, texture, and care instructions. Choose materials that suit your routine, especially in rooms used every day.

Common questions

Before you choose.

How do I choose the right size?

Measure the available floor space and leave room to walk around the piece. Check doorways, hallways, and stairs too. If you need a second opinion, send us your room dimensions through Contact.

Can I keep a list of pieces I like?

Yes. Tap Save on any product card or product page. Open the heart in the header to find your list. Favorites are saved in this browser on this device.

How do I care for my furniture?

Use a soft, dry cloth for everyday dusting, clean spills promptly, and keep furniture away from direct heat. Always follow the care instructions for the specific material.
